Car Smash

Click or tap vehicles as they roll across the screen to deal damage and rack up your score. Each vehicle takes a different number of hits to destroy — smash through 12 cars across the garage to win, and every car is wheeled into a randomly picked arena.

Accuracy and speed both matter. Deal 100% damage to a vehicle to clear it and move on. The faster you smash, the higher your final score.

How to Play

  • Attack: Click or tap a vehicle to deal damage
  • Fill the damage meter to 100% to clear each vehicle
  • 12 cars in the garage — smash them all
  • Each car arrives in a random arena: scrapyard, foundry, glacier pit and six more
  • Mobile: fully touch-controlled

About This Game

Car Smash is pure destruction satisfaction. Twelve cars roll through the garage and your job is to click them to pieces — each click deals damage, and when the meter hits 100%, the vehicle is gone and the next one rolls in. It's a tap game that rewards consistent, sustained clicking rather than reaction speed.

Why We Built It

There's a category of mobile game built entirely around the satisfaction of progressive destruction — watching a progress bar fill, seeing a visual state change, feeling like each input made a difference. Car Smash distills that to its core. The variety across 12 cars and nine randomly drawn arenas gives each smash session a different visual experience, and the increasing durability of later vehicles creates a natural pacing arc without complex level design.

How It Works

Each vehicle has a maximum HP value that increases with the vehicle index. Every click reduces HP by a fixed amount. The vehicle visual updates to show progressive damage states — dents, cracks, and deformation — at HP thresholds. When HP reaches zero, a destruction animation plays and the next vehicle spawns. Taps are tested against the car artwork itself, so a click only counts when it lands on real bodywork rather than the empty space around it. The backdrop for each car is drawn at random from nine arenas, each one aligned so the car lands squarely on its platform. The final score reflects total damage dealt and the time taken to clear all twelve cars.

Tips & Strategy

  • Click in the center of the vehicle for consistent hits. Edge clicks can occasionally miss the hitbox on fast devices.
  • Maintain a steady rhythm rather than clicking as fast as possible. Consistent pace reduces missed clicks from input lag on mobile.
  • On desktop, use a mouse click held in a hover position and focus on click rate rather than pointer movement.
  • Later vehicles have significantly more HP — don't slow down just because you cleared the first few quickly. The last two take the most time.

Ideas for Improvement

  • Weak spots — highlighted zones on each vehicle that deal double damage when hit, rewarding accurate clicking over raw speed.
  • Tools and weapons — a wrecking ball, hydraulic press, or sledgehammer selectable before each vehicle.
  • Physics debris — parts that break off and fly realistically using a simple physics engine.
  • Time attack mode — fixed vehicle HP across all runs so the only variable is how fast you can click, making it a pure speed challenge.
