Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Webgame Arcades ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit webgamearcades.com. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic usage information automatically when you visit the site, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring URL
  • IP address (used for general location and fraud prevention)

We do not require account registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or other personal information unless you contact us directly.

Cookies and Tracking

We use no analytics services and we set no cookies of our own that follow you across other websites. The only cookies that can come from a third party are advertising cookies, described below.

  • Essential storage — game scores, settings, language preference and your advertising choice are stored in your browser's local storage, not on our servers.
  • Advertising cookies — set by the advertising partners described below. Declining leaves every part of the site playable.
  • You can clear that data at any time through your browser settings.
  • Disabling cookies does not affect gameplay.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. Two kinds of ads may appear:

  • Display ads served by Google AdSense, placed alongside the page content.
  • Rewarded video ads — short videos you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. These never start on their own; nothing plays unless you ask for it, and no ad is placed over a game in progress.

Google AdSense and third-party vendors

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on this site and may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to measure ad performance and limit how often you see the same ad.
  • These partners may collect your IP address, browser and device information, and the pages you viewed here. That information is handled by each partner under its own privacy policy; we do not receive it, and we do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ad Settings.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by many other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or the NAI opt-out page.
  • You can review how Google handles data from sites that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy.
  • Opting out stops ads being personalised. It does not remove ads, and it costs you nothing on this site — every game stays fully playable.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any advertising cookie is set or any personal data is used for personalised advertising, through a consent message that meets Google's EU user consent policy and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can change your answer at any time through the Cookie settings link in the footer. Declining leaves every game fully playable; you will simply see non-personalised ads or none at all.
